How to make a Seeded Roll to help starving Animals in the winter! 🍂

You Need 🐦

  • Coconut oil or peanut butter
  • A cardboard toilet paper roll
  • A piece of string
  • Mixed seeds or nuts

Instructions 🦔

  1. Pour the mixed seeds or nuts onto a plate
  2. Take a knife and spread the outside of the toilet paper roll with coconut oil or peanut butter
  3. Make sure it is fully covered on the outside!
  4. Roll the covered cardboard roll over the seeds so they stick to it
  5. Tie a piece of string to your seeded roll
  6. Hang the seeded roll on a branch in your garden so birds and critters can eat the seeds and nuts! The coconut oil or peanut butter also gives them healthy fats to survive winter.
